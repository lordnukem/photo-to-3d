# Canon LS-88Hi III ceiling mount: printing and fitting

| file | size (mm) | volume (solid) | print on | supports |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `mount.stl` | 96 x 168 x 31 | 54 cm3 | the flat plate (as exported) | none needed; the lip is a 3.5 mm overhang |
| `turn_button.stl` | 12 x 28 x 12 | 1 cm3 | upside down: flat top on the bed, tooth up (flip it in the slicer) | none |

- PLA or PETG, 3 walls, 20 % infill. PETG if the ceiling gets warm (near a lamp, a hot attic).
- Bed: at least 100 x 170 mm.

## Before printing: measure the calculator
The fit depends on sizes read off the photos and Canon's spec. Check these, and the model can be rebuilt in a minute:
1. thickness at the keypad end, on a table (model: 9.0 mm),
2. width (89.5) and length (143),
3. height of the display end's top edge above the table (25),
4. distance from the keypad end to where the underside lifts off the table (85).

## Hardware
- 2 countersunk screws, 4 mm, with plugs that suit the ceiling (plasterboard anchors for drywall).
- 1 M3 x 16 self-tapping screw (or any 3 mm screw) and a washer: the turn button's pivot. Do not overtighten it;
  the button must turn by hand.

## Fitting
1. Screw the plate to the ceiling (the screws sit under the calculator, flush).
2. Screw the turn button onto the post and turn it sideways.
3. Hold the calculator keys down, push its display end up into the end wall under the lip, then swing the keypad
   end up against the plate.
4. Turn the button over the keypad end. Its tooth stops the calculator sliding back out of the lip.
To take it down, turn the button sideways and swing the keypad end down.
